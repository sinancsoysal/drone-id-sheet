        Questions

        What's the difference between a flyer ID and an operator ID?

        Your flyer ID (GBR-RP-…) shows you passed the CAA's online theory test. It's yours as a pilot and doesn't go on the drone. Your operator ID (GBR-OP-…) belongs to whoever is responsible for the drone, and it must be labelled on every drone they own.

        Where does the operator ID label go?

        On the main body of the drone, either visible from outside or inside a compartment you can open without tools, like the battery bay. It must be secure and safe from damage. See the full operator ID label rules.

        How big do the letters have to be?

        Block capitals taller than 3 mm. Every label on this sheet uses capitals at least 3.3 mm tall, in three sizes so one fits your drone.

        Which drones need a label?

        Since 1 January 2026, any drone of 250 g or more, and any drone of 100 g or more with a camera. Under 100 g it's optional.

        Is it really free?

        Yes. The PDF is made in your browser and costs nothing. You only pay if you want us to print the labels on waterproof vinyl, laminate your flyer ID card and post them to you.

        Are my files uploaded?

        Not for the free sheet. Your PDFs are read on your own device. They're only sent to us if you order prints. Read the privacy notice.

        Does it work for operator IDs from other countries?

        Yes. Type or paste any operator ID up to 24 characters and it's printed as you type it. Reading IDs from PDFs and printed orders are UK only for now.
