How to print your drone operator ID stickers at home

Making your sheet takes about a minute. You need the PDFs the CAA gave you when you passed the flyer ID test and registered as an operator. You can download them again from register-drones.caa.co.uk.

1. Add your IDs

Click Choose files and select your flyer ID and operator ID PDFs. You can select several at once, for example one flyer ID for everyone in the family. If you only have your operator ID written down, type or paste it into the box instead. UK operator IDs get capitals and dashes added for you. Operator IDs from other countries are printed exactly as you type them, up to 24 characters.

2. Check the preview

Make sure the names and IDs are right and the cards are cut out cleanly. Click Remove next to anything you don't need.

3. Print it yourself, or let us post it

Free: click Download PDF and print at 100% (sometimes called "Actual size"). Don't use "Fit to page". Check the 50 mm line at the bottom with a ruler, then cut along the dashed lines. Plain paper under clear tape works, but A4 waterproof vinyl sticker paper for your type of printer (inkjet or laser) gives you stickers that last.

Printed and posted: click Order and pay. You pay with Stripe and enter your UK address. We laminate your flyer ID cards, print your operator ID labels on waterproof vinyl cut ready to peel, and post them to you.

Where to put the operator ID label

The CAA says the label must be:

  • on the main body of the drone, or inside a compartment you can open without tools (like the battery bay)
  • secure, so it won't be damaged or come off
  • in block capitals, with characters taller than 3 mm

Every label on the sheet uses characters at least 3.3 mm tall. Pick the size that fits your drone. On small drones the two-line labels fit well under the battery cover. Read our summary of the label rules or the CAA's labelling guidance.

Your files stay on your device

Making the free sheet happens entirely in your browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded. They are only sent to us if you place a print order, and we delete them once your order is posted. Read the privacy notice.
