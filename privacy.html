Privacy

Making a free sheet

Your files are read and turned into a PDF inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or tracked. There are no cookies and no analytics.

Ordering a printed sheet

When you click Order and pay we upload:

  • an image of each flyer ID card on your sheet (name, flyer ID, expiry date)
  • the operator IDs you added

Stripe handles your payment and collects your name, email and delivery address. We use these only to print and post your order. We never see your card details.

As soon as your payment is confirmed, the print file goes to our print desk by email and the uploaded copies are deleted from our server. If you don't finish paying, they are deleted when the checkout expires, after an hour. We keep the order email until your order has been posted, and payment records for as long as UK accounting law requires.
